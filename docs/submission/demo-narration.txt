Most AI agent demos stop after making a decision. Radar Keeper focuses on the dangerous last mile: turning a market-risk signal into one safe, observable onchain action through KeeperHub.

The agent can use live Coinbase candles or a deterministic demo signal. It only selects protect reserve when both the price-drop and confidence thresholds are crossed.

Before execution, a hard policy layer requires a supported testnet, an allowlisted recipient, simulation of the exact request, a daily spend cap, and a closed circuit breaker. Live broadcast stays separately locked, so the normal demo cannot move funds.

This proof is real. KeeperHub landed a zero point zero zero zero zero one Sepolia Ether transaction in block eleven million three hundred seventy-nine thousand nine hundred four. The receipt shows the KeeperHub wallet, the allowlisted recipient, and successful finality.

The first client request timed out after the chain had already confirmed. Radar Keeper recovered the same execution with the same idempotency key and prevented a duplicate. Every signal, decision, policy check, simulation, recovery event, and transaction proof is preserved in the audit trail.

Radar Keeper combines agentic decisions with execution discipline: simulate first, spend within policy, recover safely, and prove every action.
